FAQ
Do I own the content and commercial license to assets I create?
Yes. All assets generated on RealSnap are fully owned by you with a commercial license included on all plans.
Do you offer free trials?
Yes — you can try RealSnap with a free trial. No credit card required to get started.
Can I cancel my subscription at any time?
Absolutely. You can cancel anytime with no penalties or lock-in periods.
What happens if I run out of monthly credits?
You can purchase additional credits at any time. Extra purchased credits do not expire, unlike the monthly renewal credits.
Do unused credits roll over?
Monthly credits reset on the 1st of each month. However, any extra credits you purchase separately will carry over indefinitely.
Does RealSnap use my data to train its AI models?
No. Your generations are fully private and are never used to train AI models.
